Research focus

My research combines physically based modelling, climate science and geospatial analysis to understand and manage hydrological extremes.

Hydroclimatic Extremes

Analysis of extreme rainfall, streamflow and drought events under a changing climate.

Climate Change Impacts

CMIP6 scenario-based assessment of hydrological response to climate change.

Hydrological Modelling

SWAT+ and HEC-HMS applications for runoff, erosion and sediment yield.

Flood & Drought Risk

Estimation and assessment of flood and drought risk at catchment scale.

Catchment Hydrology

Experimental watershed monitoring and runoff-generating area characterisation.

Remote Sensing & GIS

ArcGIS, QGIS and Google Earth Engine for spatial hydrological analysis.
